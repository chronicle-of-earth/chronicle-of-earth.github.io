Status of this preview

This site describes a project in development. Nothing here is reviewed history. The historical records that exist are machine-assisted drafts with unassessed confidence, and no historical boundary reconstruction has been published. Code and original-data licences have not been selected.

Map

The globe on the home page shows present-day Natural Earth map units (1:10m, public domain), rendered as an orthographic projection. Map units are not a list of recognised states, and their boundaries are generalised reference geometry — not historical borders and not a position on any dispute.

Typefaces

Source Serif 4 (Adobe) and Inter (Rasmus Andersson), both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and self-hosted. Licence texts ship in /fonts/.

Privacy

No accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising, fingerprinting or third-party requests. Fonts and images are served from this site.
